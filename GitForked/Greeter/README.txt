Greeter

A LOTRO (Lord of The Rings Online) Lua Plugin.


Short Description:

Greet your kinmates.


Long Description:

Greet your kinmates with one click messages.
Messages are completely customizable in: Greetings.lua and Welcomes.lua
Have as many or as few as you would like.
When a kinmate comes online or joins your kinship you will be presented with a window of the choices.
Hover the mouse over the quickslots to read the full message.
Click to post the desired message to your kinship chat.


Installation:

Unzip or 'Extact All' of the Greeter*.zip into the proper plugin folder as described below.

Installation folder for Linux or Steam:
.steam/steam/steamapps/compatdata/212500/pfx/dosdevices/c:/users/steamuser/My Documents/The Lord of the Rings Online/Plugins/

Installation folder for Windows:
c:/users/<USERNAME>/My Documents/The Lord of the Rings Online/Plugins/

If the 'Plugins' folder does not exist, create it.

The folder structure should look like:
../Plugins/GitForked/Greeter

Log in to the LOTRO game and your server, and on the right side of the character select screen click the button for 'Manage Plugins'.
Click the green refresh arrow to refresh the list of plugins installed.
Find 'Greeter' in the list and left click it.
Under 'About', select the setting for which character(s) you wish to automatically load it for.
